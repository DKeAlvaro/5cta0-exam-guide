The exam, decoded#

The 5CTA0 exam recycles a small set of question types, and sometimes the exact same question. This page shows which types come back, what they are worth, and the one-line recipe for each. Learn these and you can pass.

What this means for October 2026#

Near-certain (appeared in every recent paper)

  • A Bayes / total-probability word problem (0.2–0.3 p). Machines, scanners, tests. Recipe.
  • A spectral factorisation (0.25–0.75 p): given \(P_x(e^{j\theta})\) as a fraction of cosines, find \(L(z)\) and \(\sigma_i^2\). Choose the root inside the unit circle. Recipe.
  • Derive an MLE and a MAP (2–3 p together). Same 4 steps every time: log, derivative, set to 0, solve. MLE · MAP.
  • The Part 3 chain (~2 p): given \(r[0], r[\pm1], r[\pm2]\) → correlogram → AR by Yule–Walker → (MA(1)) → which plot is right. Chain.
  • A likelihood ratio test (1–2 p): ratio of the two PDFs, take the log, isolate \(x\), watch the direction of the inequality. LRT.

Very likely

  • A stationarity/ergodicity MC. The dice question appeared twice with the same answer (b) (Jan 2022 1c, Apr 2025 1d).
  • Windowing / zero-padding / Bartlett / Welch theory (MC or a matching table).
  • A Bayesian detection question with priors and costs.
  • A joint PDF: find \(c\), a marginal, a conditional or check independence.

Recipe card (one line per type)#

Type The move
Bayes word problem \(P(B)=\sum_i P(B\mid A_i)P(A_i)\), then \(P(A_k\mid B)=P(B\mid A_k)P(A_k)/P(B)\)
Normalise a joint PDF \(\iint p=1\) over the region; draw the region to get the limits
Marginal integrate out the other variable over its range for fixed value of the one you keep
Independence \(p_{X,Y}=p_Xp_Y\) and the support is a rectangle
WSS mean constant and \(r(t_1,t_2)\) depends only on \(t_2-t_1\)
Ergodic (in the mean) time average of one realisation = ensemble mean
Spectral factorisation \(\cos\theta=\frac{z+z^{-1}}{2}\), match \(C(1+a^2)\) and \(Ca\), keep \(\lvert a\rvert<1\), \(\sigma_i^2=C_n/C_d\)
MLE \(\ln L\), \(\partial/\partial\theta=0\), solve, (check 2nd derivative < 0)
MAP same, but on \(\ln p(x\mid\theta)+\ln p(\theta)\); conjugate priors just add exponents
MMSE \(\int\theta\,p(\theta\mid x)\,d\theta\) (posterior mean)
CRLB \(I(\theta)=-E[\partial^2\ln p/\partial\theta^2]\), bound \(=1/I\); not valid if the support depends on \(\theta\)
Correlogram \(P=r[0]+2\sum_k r[k]\cos k\theta\)
AR(p) Yule–Walker: \(\mathbf R\mathbf a=-\mathbf r\), \(\sigma_i^2=r[0]+\sum a_kr[k]\), \(P=\sigma_i^2/\lvert A(e^{j\theta})\rvert^2\)
MA(1) \(\sigma^2(1+b^2)=r[0]\), \(\sigma^2b=r[1]\), keep \(\lvert b\rvert<1\); \(P=r[0]+2r[1]\cos\theta\)
Pick the plot evaluate each PSD at \(\theta=0\) and \(\theta=\pi\)
LRT \(\frac{p(x\mid H_1)}{p(x\mid H_0)}\gtrless\lambda\), log, isolate \(x\); dividing by a negative flips the inequality
\(P_{FA}\), \(P_D\) integrate \(p(x\mid H_0)\) resp. \(p(x\mid H_1)\) over the region where you decide \(H_1\)
Bayes threshold \(\lambda=\frac{(C_{10}-C_{00})P_0}{(C_{01}-C_{11})P_1}\)

What changed over the years#

  • 2019–2021 (different lecturers): erf tables, Wiener filters, Gauss–Newton, t-statistics, exam split in 4–5 exercises. Some of this is no longer in the reader (the Jan 2022 key itself says nonlinear LS is "no longer part of the content"). It is still solved here because the ideas come back.
  • 2022 onwards (current team): 4 blocks with the point split above, MC + open questions on Ans, recycled questions.
  • 2024 onwards: more "final answer only" boxes in Part 1 and Part 3, conjugate priors (Beta, Gamma) in Part 2, "pick the right plot" in Part 3, applied stories in Part 4 (genAI detector, wireless sensor).
