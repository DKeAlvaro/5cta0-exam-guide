A4 cheat sheet#

You may bring one A4, both sides, own notes. This page prints as a dense A4 (Ctrl/Cmd + P → A4, scale "fit", margins minimal; it fits on two sides). Better still: copy it by hand and add your own traps, writing it is half the studying.

1 · Probability & signals#

Total prob. \(P(B)=\sum_iP(B\mid A_i)P(A_i)\) · Bayes \(P(A_k\mid B)=\frac{P(B\mid A_k)P(A_k)}{P(B)}\) "pass 85 %" → defect 0.15 · priors from counts: divide by total. Independent: \(P(A\mid B)=P(A)\), \(P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)\); disjoint ≠ independent; \(P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\).

RVs. discrete = countable (counts); continuous = measurements, averages. \(E[X]=\sum xp(x)\) (weighted avg, long-run mean). \(\text{Var}=E[X^2]-\mu^2\). Cov: sign = direction, unbounded, unit-dependent; \(\rho\in[-1,1]\). Binomial \(\binom nkp^k(1-p)^{n-k}\). Exponential memoryless. Joint PDF: draw region → limits. Normalise \(\iint p=1\). Marginal \(p_X=\int p\,dy\) (y over region at that x). Conditional \(p_{X\mid Y}=p_{XY}/p_Y\) on the slice. Independent ⇔ factorises and rectangle. Diagonal cov → axis-aligned ellipses; tilted ⇒ cov ≠ 0.

WSS: constant mean + \(r(t_1,t_2)\) depends on lag only. Ergodic: time avg of 1 realisation = ensemble avg (needs stationarity). Something drawn once per realisation (amplitude \(A\)) ⇒ not ergodic in what depends on it. i.i.d. dice ⇒ ergodic mean & var. \(\cos(t+U)\): WSS, ergodic; \(A\cos t\): not WSS. \(\cos\alpha\cos\beta=\frac12[\cos(\alpha+\beta)+\cos(\alpha-\beta)]\). Autocorr: \(r[-l]=r[l]\), \(r[0]\ge|r[l]|\), PSD \(\ge0\). White: \(\sigma^2\delta[l]\). \(\hat r_{xy}[l]=\frac1N\sum x[n]y[n-l]\).

Spectral factorisation. \(P_y=|H|^2P_x\); \(P=\sigma_i^2L(z)L(z^{-1})\), \(L\) causal, stable, min phase, \(l[0]=1\). \((1\mp az^{-1})(1\mp az)=(1+a^2)\mp2a\cos\theta\); \(\cos\theta=\frac{z+z^{-1}}2\). \(\alpha\mp\beta\cos\theta\): \(C(1+a^2)=\alpha\), \(2Ca=\beta\) → roots \(a,1/a\) → keep \(|a|<1\) → \(C=\beta/2a\). \(\sigma_i^2=C_n/C_d\). Check at \(\theta=0\).

2 · Estimation#

MLE: \(\ln L=\sum\ln p(x_n;\theta)\) → \(\partial_\theta=0\) → solve → check \(\partial^2<0\). Asympt. unbiased, efficient, Gaussian \(\mathcal N(\theta,I^{-1})\); invariance \(\widehat{g(\theta)}=g(\hat\theta)\); maximise over θ, not x. Rayleigh \(\frac x\theta e^{-x^2/2\theta}\) → \(\frac1{2N}\sum x^2\); \(\frac{x}{\theta^2}e^{-x^2/2\theta^2}\) → \(\sqrt{\cdot}\) of that; exp. \(\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\) → \(1/\bar x\); Gamma(k,θ) → \(\bar x/k\). Sigmoid \(\lambda(\theta)\): \(\lambda'=\lambda(1-\lambda)\); solve for λ, \(\theta=\ln\frac{\lambda}{1-\lambda}\), need \(0<\lambda<1\).

CRLB: \(I=-E[\partial^2\ln p]=E[(\partial\ln p)^2]>0\); \(\text{Var}\ge1/I\); i.i.d. \(I=N\,i(\theta)\); efficient = equals bound; equality ⇔ \(\partial\ln p=I(\theta)(g(\mathbf x)-\theta)\). Not valid if support depends on θ (regularity). Rayleigh \(E[x^2]=2\sigma^2\).

Bayes: post ∝ lik × prior. MMSE = mean, MAP = mode (uniform cost), median (abs). MMSE=MAP for symmetric unimodal. \(\int_0^\infty\theta^ne^{-s\theta}d\theta=\frac{n!}{s^{n+1}}\). Beta(α,β)+\(\theta^A(1-\theta)^B\) → Beta(α+A, β+B), mode \(\frac{\alpha+A-1}{\alpha+\beta+A+B-2}\). Gamma(α,β)+Poisson (\(\lambda^Se^{-n\lambda}\)) → Gamma(α+S, β+n), mode \(\frac{\alpha+S-1}{\beta+n}\), MLE \(S/n\). Binary \(X=\pm1\) + Gaussian: MMSE \(\tanh(y/\sigma^2)\), MAP sign(y).

LS: \(\min\sum(x-s(\theta))^2\), no noise pdf. \(\hat\theta=(H^TH)^{-1}H^Tx\); error ⟂ col(H); need \(N\ge K\); BLUE: \(W=C_x^{-1}\). Line: \((\sum x^2)a+(\sum x)b=\sum xy\), \((\sum x)a+nb=\sum y\). Nonlinear: transform (\(\alpha=\ln\theta\) → \(\hat\theta=e^{\bar x}\)), else Gauss–Newton.

3 · Spectral estimation#

Energy \(E<\infty\), \(P=0\) (pulses). Power \(0\lt P<\infty\), \(E=\infty\) (periodic: \(A^2/2\); \(\cos t\,u(t)\): ¼). Window: main lobe → resolution (≈2π/N); side lobes → leakage. Rect: narrowest lobe, −13 dB; triang −27, Hann −32 (less leakage, worse resolution). Longer N → better resolution. Zero padding: samples FTD at \(2\pi k/L\), no extra resolution. DFT = FTD at \(2\pi k/N\).

Raw periodogram \(\frac1N|\text{DFT}|^2\) = correlogram (biased \(\hat r\)): asympt. unbiased (triangular smoothing), Var ≈ \(P^2\) → not consistent. Bartlett: K non-overlapping, Var ≈ \(P^2/K\), bias↑ (resol. 2π/M). Welch: overlap + window, Var↓ but not 1/K, bias less than Bartlett; \(K=\frac{N-L}{D}+1\). BT: window \(\hat r\), Var ∝ 1/N → consistent.

Models (minus sign on a's!): MA \(x=i+\sum b_ki[n-k]\); AR \(x=i-\sum a_kx[n-k]\). \(P=\sigma^2|B|^2/|A|^2\). \(|1+ce^{-j\theta}|^2=1+c^2+2c\cos\theta\); \(|1+a_1e^{-j\theta}+a_2e^{-2j\theta}|^2=1+a_1^2+a_2^2+2(a_1+a_1a_2)\cos\theta+2a_2\cos2\theta\); \(\cos2\theta=2\cos^2\theta-1\). MA(q): \(r[l]=\sigma^2\sum b_kb_{k-l}\), 0 for \(|l|>q\). AR(1): \(r[l]=\frac{\sigma^2}{1-a^2}(-a)^{|l|}\). ARMA(1,1) \(x=i+bi[n-1]-ax[n-1]\): \(r_0=\sigma^2(1+b(b-a))-ar_1\), \(r_1=\sigma^2b-ar_0\), \(r_l=-ar_{l-1}\).

Chain. Correlogram \(r_0+2\sum r_k\cos k\theta\) (may go <0). YW: Toeplitz\([r]\,[1,a_1..a_p]^T=[\sigma^2,0..0]^T\). AR(1): \(a_1=-r_1/r_0\), \(\sigma^2=r_0+a_1r_1\). AR(2): \(r_0a_1+r_1a_2=-r_1\), \(r_1a_1+r_0a_2=-r_2\), \(\sigma^2=r_0+a_1r_1+a_2r_2\). MA(1): \(r_1b^2-r_0b+r_1=0\), keep \(|b|<1\), \(\sigma^2=r_1/b\), PSD \(=r_0+2r_1\cos\theta\). Pick plot: evaluate at θ = 0 and π; AR/MA never < 0. Order: residual var ↓ always (overfit); AIC \(=N\ln\hat\sigma^2+2P\), FPE \(=\frac{N+P}{N-P}\hat\sigma^2\) → min. Any PSD ≈ AR of high order.

4 · Detection#

decide ↓ / true → \(H_0\) \(H_1\)
\(H_0\) TN miss (II)
\(H_1\) FA (I) detect

\(P_{FA}=\int_{X_1}p(x|H_0)\), \(P_D=\int_{X_1}p(x|H_1)\), \(P_M=1-P_D\). NP: fix \(P_{FA}\), max \(P_D\) → LRT. ROC: \(P_D\) vs \(P_{FA}\), above diagonal; swaps → below.

LRT: \(\frac{p(x|H_1)}{p(x|H_0)}\gtrless\lambda\); i.i.d. → products; ln; isolate data term; ÷ negative flips H₀/H₁. λ=1 → curves cross. Mean shift → \(\bar x\) big. Var ↑ → \(\sum x^2\) big. Narrower Laplace → \(\sum|x|\) small. Exp \(\lambda_1>\lambda_0\) → x small. Rayleigh σ↑ → x big (\(P_{FA}=e^{-\gamma^2/2\sigma_0^2}\), \(P_D=P_{FA}^{\sigma_0^2/\sigma_1^2}\)). Gauss vs \(\frac14e^{-|y|/2}\): \(|y|>1.588\), \(P_D=0.45\), \(P_{FA}=2Q=0.11\).

Tails: \(P(X>t)=Q(\frac{t-\mu}{\sigma})\); \(Q(0)=.5\), \(Q(1)=.159\), \(Q(2)=.023\), \(Q(3)=.0013\); \(Q=\frac12[1-\text{erf}(z/\sqrt2)]\). Exp: \(\int_a^b\lambda e^{-\lambda x}=e^{-\lambda a}-e^{-\lambda b}\). Bayes: \(\lambda=\frac{(C_{10}-C_{00})P_0}{(C_{01}-C_{11})P_1}\), \(C_{ij}\) = decide i, truth j. 0/1 costs → \(P_0/P_1\), min \(P_e\); equal priors → ML. Posteriors: \(H_1\) iff \(C_{01}P(H_1|x)>C_{10}P(H_0|x)\). \(P_0\)↑ → λ↑. Matched filter: \(\sum x_ns_n\gtrless\sigma^2\ln\lambda+\frac12\sum s_n^2\); \(h[n]=s[N-1-n]\); max SNR; correlated noise → prewhiten.
