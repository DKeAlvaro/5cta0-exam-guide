| $\mathcal{H}_0$ | 1 | 2 |
| $\mathcal{H}_1$ | 3 | 4 |

- (a) 1= true negative, 2 = false negative/miss/type II error, 3 = false positive/false alarm/type I error, 4 = true positive/detection
