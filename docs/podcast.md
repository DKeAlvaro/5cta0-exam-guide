# Podcast: the exam in 5 episodes

<p class="lead">Ava explains, Ben asks the questions you would ask. Every formula appears on screen while they talk. In English, like the exam.</p>

!!! recipe key "How to use it (this is what makes it work)"
    - When a purple **YOUR TURN** box appears, answer **out loud or on paper before the countdown ends**. Pause the video if you need more time. Just listening is far less effective than answering first.
    - After each episode, do the **Your turn** question of the matching lesson on paper, without looking.
    - Episode 4 (true or false) is the one to replay on Monday morning.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Detection: the likelihood ratio test](#ep1) | [4.1](e4-lrt.md), [4.2](e4-bayes.md) | in 9 of 9 exams, most mechanical |
| 2 | [Spectral: correlogram, AR, MA](#ep2) | [3.1](e3-corr.md), [3.2](e3-arma.md) | about 2 points, pure arithmetic |
| 3 | [Estimation: MLE and MAP](#ep3) | [2.1](e2-mle.md), [2.2](e2-map.md), [2.4](e2-facts.md) | the biggest block |
| 4 | [Rapid-fire true or false](#ep4) | [1.3](e1-wss.md), [2.4](e2-facts.md), [3.3](e3-facts.md), [4.2](e4-bayes.md) | the multiple-choice sentences |
