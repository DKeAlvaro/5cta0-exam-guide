## How a lesson works

- **Figures you can play with.** They animate once by themselves when you scroll to them. Then drag the sliders, press the buttons, read the sentence under the figure: it changes with what you do.
- **Worked solutions come one step at a time.** Before pressing *Next step*, guess what the next step is. That is the exercise.
- **"Check your answer" is closed.** Do the question on paper first, then open it.
- **True/false cards**: press *True* or *False*, then read why. These are the exact sentences the multiple-choice questions recycle.
- Press **Mark as done** at the bottom of each lesson to tick it off in the menu.
- Stuck on a sentence? Select it and press **Ask about this** (bottom-right **Ask** button; it uses your own API key).
